Questions

Good to know before you visit.

Do you accept my insurance?

We work with most major insurers and can submit claims on your behalf. Bring your card to your first visit and we’ll check your cover before any treatment starts.

Will it hurt?

Our approach is built around comfort: numbing gel, gentle technique, and a signal to pause at any time. Many of our patients arrive anxious and tell us it was the easiest visit they’ve had.

How soon can I be seen?

Most new patients are seen within a week, and we hold same-day slots for dental emergencies. Message or call and we’ll find the earliest time.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We see families, with longer, gentler appointments for children and a relaxed first-visit introduction for nervous little ones.

What does a first visit include?

A forty-minute exam with your dentist, digital X-rays, a clear explanation of what we find, and a written plan with prices. There’s no obligation to start treatment.
