WEBSITE · BRAND · CONCEPT

Bloom Café

A warm, editorial website and packaging for a neighbourhood café.

Challenge

Neighbourhood cafés lose orders to apps because their own sites are slow to read, hard to order from and look nothing like the shop.

Solution

A one-screen story with a single action, order for pickup, backed by real food photography, a warm serif voice and a menu that scans in seconds on a phone. The same identity runs onto cups and bags.

Key decisions

  • One action above the fold
  • Warmth through shape
  • Packaging as part of the site

Northwind Studio

A quiet, image-led website for an architecture and interiors studio.

Challenge

Architecture sites often bury the work under heavy menus and long text, so visitors never feel the buildings.

Solution

  • An editorial serif

Halo Skincare

A calm, minimalist identity, website and packaging for a luxury skincare line.

Challenge

Luxury skincare is crowded with loud claims. A new label earns trust by looking calm, precise and unhurried.

Solution

A quiet system: a single wide-set wordmark, ink-on-bone typography, matte porcelain packaging with brushed-metal caps, and a website where the product has the whole screen.

Key decisions

  • Say less
  • Materials do the talking
  • Space as a feature

WEBSITE · APP · CONCEPT

Orbit Fitness

A high-energy website and app screen for a 30-day fitness challenge.

Challenge

Fitness brands need to create momentum fast. People decide in seconds whether to join and keep going.

Solution

A dark interface with one electric accent, huge condensed headlines, a real-member spotlight and a class schedule you can book in one tap. The app turns progress into a streak ring.

Key decisions

  • One loud colour
  • Real people, real effort
  • Progress you can see

Pulse Records

A bold website and music player for an independent record label.

Challenge

An independent label needs a home that sounds like its music: loud, a little strange and built for listening, not reading.

Solution

Summit Co.

An outdoor-gear storefront and social posts built around the trail.
